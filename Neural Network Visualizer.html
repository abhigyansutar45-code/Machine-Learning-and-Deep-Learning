A sample enters through x₁ and x₂. Every neuron computes a weighted sum plus a bias, applies its activation function, and sends that signal onward. The final sigmoid value becomes a probability for Class 1.
The predicted probability is compared with the expected label to calculate loss. Gradients travel backward from the output through each connection. Gradient descent then nudges weights and biases by the selected learning rate to reduce future error.
